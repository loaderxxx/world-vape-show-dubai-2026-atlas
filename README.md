# World Vape Show Dubai 2026
## Independent, source-linked exhibitor atlas

**10–12 November 2026 · Dubai World Trade Centre · Research snapshot: 10 November 2026**

A neutral reference for understanding the event's published exhibitor landscape. This project records what sources say, separates brands from legal entities, and makes uncertainty visible. It is not an official event publication, a supplier recommendation, or a product-safety assessment.

### Start here

- [Exhibitor data](data/exhibitors.json): all **138 live website entries**, including stand and available organizer profile.
- [Source reconciliation](reports/source-reconciliation.md): differences between the website and the printed catalogue.
- Company-level research and an evidence-quality cross-analysis are in progress. Do not interpret an unfinished profile as a negative finding.

### What is verified

The current website was re-read and compared with the 9 November snapshot: names, stands and profile URLs are unchanged. Catalogue differences are retained rather than silently resolved. Company descriptions will be source-linked, concise and explicitly qualified when based on self-description or uncertain identity matching.

### Important navigation discrepancy

**Vape Dinner Lady** is listed at **1220** on the website and **The Pavilion** in the printed catalogue. Confirm with the organizer before relying on either location.

### Scope and safeguards

- Public information only. No private plans, registration records, personal notes, credentials or unpublished contact information.
- Listing is not proof of legal identity, authorization, certification or attendance on the day.
- No purchasing rankings, sales leads, pricing, product recommendations or endorsements.
- No participant outreach has been performed.
- Original descriptions are paraphrased; source publications and logos are not redistributed.
- Research is a dated snapshot. Corrections should cite a source and preserve conflicting evidence.
