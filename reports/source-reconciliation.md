## Interpretation rules

- Preserve the organizer's display name separately from any proposed legal entity.
- Prefer neither source blindly when locations conflict; check signage or organizer information.
- Do not infer a hall from a stand-number prefix alone.
- Distinguish an accessible organizer profile, a company's self-description, a search-result snippet and independent verification.
- Treat certification and product-safety statements as unverified unless the underlying current document and relevant scope have been checked.
